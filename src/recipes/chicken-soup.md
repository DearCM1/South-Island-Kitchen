## Method

### **Preparation**

1. Wash and drain the rice.
2. Peel and dice the **carrots**, **parsnips**, **celery**, **leek**, and **onion** and leave in a prep bowl. Remove the skins of the garlic and leave separate.

### **Cooking**

3. Heat the **olive oil** in a stock pot (6 L or bigger) over medium heat and add the bulk vegeables, cook for **5–6 minutes**, stirring frequently, layer in some **salt**.
4. Add the **garlic**, more **salt**, and **pepper** (Note 1).
5. Add the **wild rice**, **thyme**, **tarragon**, **bay leaves**, and **broth**. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat to low and cover the pot.
6. Simmer for **30 minutes**, stirring occasionally.
7. Add the **chicken**, re-cover, and simmer for a further **20 minutes**, or until the chicken is fully cooked and the rice is tender (Note 2).
8. Remove the chicken to a chopping board with tongs and shred it with two forks (Note 3).
9. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and simmer for a further **1–2 minutes**.

### **Service**

10. Remove the **thyme** and **tarragon** sprigs and the **bay leaves**.
11. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the **red wine vinegar** and **parsley**. Taste and adjust the salt and vinegar (Note 4).
12. Garnish with extra parsley and black pepper before serving.

---

## Notes

> Note 1: Add the garlic after the other vegetables, not before, so it doesn't burn.

> Note 2: Wild rice takes longer than the chicken to cook, so it goes in first. If the rice still has a hard bite, keep simmering and add a splash of water or stock if needed.

> Note 3: Add the vinegar off the heat so its brightness isn't cooked away. Start with 1 tablespoon and add more to taste. The rice will keep absorbing stock as the soup sits, so loosen leftovers with a little water or stock when reheating.
